The agent writes down what it learns about each site. Those notes used to stop at twelve, the oldest dropped - often what still held, such as how the form goes. Now, at twelve, the model writes them (with what was distilled before) into one account of how the site is worked, by stage, without personal details; what holds for every site on its recruiting system goes up to that system's notes. The agent reads the account first, then what was noted since. Analytics → Site notes shows the account, the notes that went into it, and lets you reword it. Sites with many notes from before are distilled after the update, in the background.
