The program read only the last three days of mail, every hour - so what came before it first read your mailbox was never read: assessments, interviews and rejections of your earliest applications were in your inbox and not in the program. Now, once, the mail is read back to two weeks before your first application (and again if older applications are brought in). What it finds goes on the applications - their stages and their mails, dated when the mails came, for the analysis - without notices, without cards for what is over, and nothing on the calendar for dates that have passed. An old email about an interview, an assessment or an offer that matches no application gets a card to place it.
