Analytics → Automation: under how each run ended, the runs that stopped for you are split by what they waited for - a sign-in only you can do, a code, something in the window, your confirmation, an answer. A task that failed because this computer had no connection (the network or the proxy) is counted as that, no longer as an error in the program. And when checking your mail or scanning postings fails three times in a row, a card says so on the to-do page; it closes by itself when it works again.
