An hour after an interview began, or at an assessment's deadline, a card on the to-do page asks whether it took place. Saying yes marks it done on the calendar and moves the application to that stage when it was not there yet; you can also say how it went (well, so-so, not well) and add a word for later. The answer shows on the application's time line, for your own look back. Interviews and assessments that were already over before (read back from old mail) are not asked about.
