# SmartRecruiters

- No account. "I'm interested" opens page 1 (Easy Apply, personal information, experience, education, profiles,
  résumé, message to the hiring team); "Next" opens the screening questions with the consent boxes and "Submit".
- The whole form is inside shadow roots. The controls are listed all the same; if something does not answer to a
  click by its number, click the point you see in the picture.
- Easy Apply (upload the résumé at the top) fills contact, experience and education well. It leaves "Confirm your
  e-mail" empty, and opens an edit form with an empty required title for each project it took for a job: cancel it,
  then delete the entry (a dialog asks to confirm).
- Experience descriptions refuse ";". An entry stays in edit mode until saved, and "Next" scrolls back to it.
- Month/year pickers: a year header with arrows and a grid of months.
- Going back from the screening page clears the screening answers.
