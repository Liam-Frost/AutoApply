# SAP SuccessFactors

- The employer's careers site hands over to a careerNN.sapsf.* or successfactors address: the account and the
  application live there. An account is needed (e-mail, password twice, name, country, a privacy statement to accept).
- Password rules are strict and stated on the page (often 8-18 characters): pass them on when a password is made.
- One long page with collapsible sections: profile information, documents (résumé, cover letter, other attachments),
  work experience and education added entry by entry, language skills, the job's own questions, a signature and date.
- The résumé is not parsed: experience and education are entered by hand. Dates want a full date: say in the
  report which day you chose when only a month is known.
- "Save" keeps a draft ("The draft application was saved successfully"); "Apply" at the bottom sends it.
