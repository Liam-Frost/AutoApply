# Workday

- One account per employer (each tenant its own): sign in or create it before the form opens. The e-mail may need
  confirming from a mail the site sends.
- A wizard: My Information, My Experience, Application Questions, Voluntary Disclosures, Self Identify, Review. Each
  step is saved with "Save and Continue"; the Review step has the button that sends.
- "Autofill with Resume" is on the first page; when the account already holds data it may parse nothing. Check what
  the account kept from an earlier application: answers given for another role are still there and may be wrong here.
- Work experience and education are added entry by entry ("Add"); dates are month/year boxes that take typing.
- Lists are buttons that open a panel of options; some search as you type. Check the value after choosing.
- "Back" asks "Discard application?": what was saved with Save and Continue stays; only the open step is dropped.
- A session that sat idle for long may have expired: sign in again and the draft is still there ("My Applications",
  "Candidate Home").
