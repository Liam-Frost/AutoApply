# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Its full text applies; this page says what that means here and whom to tell when something goes wrong.

## The short version

Everyone who takes part in this project — in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and anywhere else the project
is represented — is expected to make it a place where others can take part without being harassed.

**Do**

- Be patient and considerate. People come here with different experience, different first languages (the project is
  maintained in English and Chinese) and, often, in the middle of a stressful job search.
- Criticise the idea or the code, not the person. Say what you ran, what you saw and what you expected.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and say why you disagree if you do, once.
- Give credit, and own your mistakes.

**Do not**

- Insult, demean or harass anyone, or make sexualised remarks or advances.
- Publish someone's private information: their name, address, employer, application records or anything from their
  profile. Screenshots and logs attached to an issue must not contain other people's data, or your own if you would
  not want it public.
- Use the project to pressure, impersonate or deceive: no requests for help applying on someone else's behalf without
  their knowledge, bypassing a site's protections, or fabricating qualifications. Those are also things the program
  itself refuses to do.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, tell the maintainer privately:

- through GitHub's private report form, [**Report a vulnerability**](https://github.com/Liam-Frost/AutoApply/security/advisories/new),
  choosing any title that starts with "Conduct:" (it reaches only the maintainer); or
- at the e-mail address given in the [LICENSE](LICENSE) file.

Say what happened, where (a link if there is one) and when. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainer reads every report and decides what is fair: a private word, a public correction, removing a comment, a
temporary or a permanent ban from the project's spaces, in proportion to what happened and whether it has happened
before. The enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant are the reference.
